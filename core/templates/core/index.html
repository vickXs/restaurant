Our Culinary Ethos

Rooted in Tradition, Elevated by Craft

Founded with a passion for authentic Nigerian flavors, Buka & Bistro reimagines classic street and home-cooked staples into exquisite dining artistry. Every bell pepper, stockfish cut, and yam tuber is sourced directly from certified local farmers who share our commitment to sustainability and supreme quality.
